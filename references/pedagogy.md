## Skills and language elements

Plan every lesson around both layers:

| Layer | Items | How they appear in a lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Four skills (الْمَهَارَاتُ اللُّغَوِيَّةُ) | اِسْتِمَاعٌ (menyimak), كَلَامٌ (berbicara), قِرَاءَةٌ (membaca), كِتَابَةٌ (menulis) | each exercise is tagged with one skill |
| Language elements (الْعَنَاصِرُ اللُّغَوِيَّةُ) | أَصْوَاتٌ (sounds), مُفْرَدَاتٌ (vocabulary), تَرَاكِيبُ / قَوَاعِدُ (structures/grammar) | taught inside the skills, not as isolated theory |

Principles:
- Teach grammar functionally. Pick the point from the dialogue or text, show it in
  context, then practise it. Avoid long theoretical explanations at beginner level.
- For beginners, sequence oral before written: listen → speak → read → write.
- Have the teacher model and use Arabic for classroom language (instructions,
  greetings, praise), with the explanation language as support.
- Include a short sound (أَصْوَاتٌ) note whenever the theme's words contain
  sounds that are hard for Indonesian learners (see arabic-text-rules.md §Learner errors).
- Add one cultural or values touch where natural, such as an adab expression or
  a local or Islamic context. Keep it short.

## Lesson flow

Default flow for one meeting (Pendahuluan → Inti → Penutup):

1. **Pendahuluan (≈10–15%)**: salam, a trigger question (pertanyaan pemantik),
   a quick link to prior knowledge, and the goals stated in simple words.
2. **Inti (≈70–80%)**, in three moves:
   - *Memahami*: present vocab with pictures or realia, listen and repeat, then
     listen to the dialogue or text.
   - *Mengaplikasi*: role-play the dialogue, discover the grammar point from
     examples, do controlled exercises, then a freer speaking or writing task.
   - *Merefleksi*: the learner checks their own work against the key or rubric.
3. **Penutup (≈10%)**: a short formative check (exit ticket of 2–3 items),
   reflection, and a preview of the next meeting.

For longer units, the genre-based cycle also works: build knowledge of the
field → model text → joint construction → independent construction.

When the class spans several meetings, split the lesson so that each meeting
ends with a formative check.

## Level orientation (CEFR / ACTFL)

This is a rough orientation, not an official equivalence. Use it only to pitch
task difficulty.

| Skill level | Learner can… | Approx. CEFR | Approx. ACTFL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner | use familiar words and formulaic phrases; ask and answer about self, home, school | A1–A2 | Novice |
| Intermediate | take part in short conversations on predictable topics, handle simple sentences and transactions | A2–B1 | Intermediate |
| Advanced | narrate and describe across time frames in paragraph-length speech or text | B1–B2+ | Advanced |

Task implications:
- Beginner tasks are word and sentence level: matching, labelling, fill-in with a
  word bank, scripted dialogue, guided description.
- Intermediate tasks are sentence-to-short-paragraph level: information gaps,
  short answers, guided paragraphs, role-plays with one unexpected turn.
- Advanced tasks are paragraph level: summarising, comparing texts, opinion with
  reasons, handling a complication in a role-play.

## Curriculum alignment (madrasah, Indonesia)

Use this only if the teacher asks for alignment with the madrasah curriculum.

- The current Capaian Pembelajaran (CP) for Bahasa Arab in madrasah is the
  Keputusan Dirjen Pendis No. 9941 Tahun 2025 (28 November 2025). It is the
  reference for planning, teaching and assessment at MI, MTs and MA/MAK. It
  replaced earlier CP decrees (No. 3211/2022 and No. 3302/2024). If the teacher
  cites a newer decree, follow theirs.
- CP elements: **Komponen Bahasa** (sounds, vocabulary, sentence patterns or grammar)
  and **Keterampilan Bahasa** (menyimak, berbicara, membaca–memirsa,
  menulis–mempresentasikan).
- Three competences: berbahasa (kifāyah lughawiyyah), berkomunikasi
  (kifāyah ittiṣāliyyah), berbudaya (kifāyah thaqāfiyyah).
- Phases: Fase A (kelas 1–2 MI), B (3–4 MI), C (5–6 MI), D (7–9 MTs),
  E (10 MA), F (11–12 MA). Example: Fase A covers themes such as perkenalan,
  keluargaku, rumahku and madrasahku.
- The CP lists approaches including deep learning (pembelajaran mendalam),
  communicative, humanistic, lexical, structural and genre-based. It also
  integrates "nilai-nilai cinta", such as love of Allah and His Messenger
  through the language of the Qur'an and Hadith, love of knowledge, and
  polite, empathetic language.
- Do not quote CP sentences or invent CP wording. Say "selaras dengan elemen
  Komponen Bahasa dan Keterampilan Bahasa pada CP Bahasa Arab madrasah" and let
  the teacher paste the exact CP text.
- The learner level (beginner/intermediate/advanced) is not the same as the fase.
  A Fase D class can still be beginners. Ask or assume, and state the assumption.

## Modul ajar components

Minimum components (Panduan Pembelajaran dan Asesmen, Kemendikbudristek):
1. Tujuan pembelajaran
2. Langkah-langkah / kegiatan pembelajaran
3. Rencana asesmen awal and akhir, with instruments and scoring
4. Media pembelajaran (bahan bacaan, lembar kerja, video, links)

Fuller version, used when the teacher asks for a "modul ajar lengkap":

| Informasi umum | Komponen inti | Lampiran |
|---|---|---|
| identitas modul, kompetensi awal, profil/karakter yang dikembangkan*, sarana-prasarana, target peserta didik, model pembelajaran | tujuan, asesmen, pemahaman bermakna, pertanyaan pemantik, kegiatan pembelajaran, refleksi | LKPD, pengayaan & remedial, bahan bacaan, glosarium, daftar pustaka |

\* Use the profile or character framework the teacher's madrasah uses. Do not
assume one.

Mapping from this skill's output: goals → tujuan; vocab, dialogue and grammar →
bahan bacaan and kegiatan inti; exercises → LKPD and asesmen; rubric → instrumen
penilaian; teacher notes → langkah kegiatan with timing; extension task →
pengayaan; simplified version → remedial.

## Structural models from established materials

Use these only for structure. Never copy their texts or exercises.

- *al-ʿArabiyyah bayna Yadayk*: a unit opens with dialogues and their
  vocabulary, then vocabulary practice, structures, sounds and listening,
  speaking, reading, and writing. Lesson order: whole (dialogue) → parts
  (elements) → skills.
- Rushdi Ahmad Ṭuʿaymah, *Taʿlīm al-ʿArabiyyah li-ghayr al-nāṭiqīn bihā:
  manāhijuhu wa asālībuh* (ISESCO, 1989): a standard methodology reference for
  teaching Arabic to non-native speakers. Cite it as background, not for specific claims.
