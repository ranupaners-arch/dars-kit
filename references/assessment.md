## Exercise bank by skill

Tag each exercise with its skill and cognitive level (C1–C6).

| Skill | Exercise (Arabic instruction) | Typical level | Notes |

| اِسْتِمَاعٌ | اِسْتَمِعْ وَأَشِرْ / ضَعْ عَلَامَةَ (✓): listen and tick a word or picture | C1–C2 | give the teacher's script in the key |
| اِسْتِمَاعٌ | اِسْتَمِعْ وَأَجِبْ: listen to the dialogue and answer true/false | C2 | statements must be clearly true or false |

| قِرَاءَةٌ | اِخْتَرِ الْإِجَابَةَ الصَّحِيحَةَ: 3-option multiple choice | C2–C4 | see item-writing rules |

In the masculine/feminine imperatives (تَحَدَّثْ / تَحَدَّثِي, اُكْتُبْ / اُكْتُبِي),
address the class with the masculine plural (اِسْتَمِعُوا) or the generic
masculine singular, unless the class is all-female (then use اِسْتَمِعِي /
اِسْتَمِعْنَ). Be consistent within a worksheet.

## Item-writing rules

Based on the classroom item-writing guidelines of Haladyna, Downing and Rodriguez (2002):
- Test one idea per item. Put the main idea in the stem, not in the options.
- Use **3 options** (1 key + 2 distractors) by default. Three options are
  sufficient in most cases, and a weak fourth option adds little.
- Make every distractor plausible, ideally based on typical learner errors.
- Keep options homogeneous in content and grammatical form, and about equal in length.
- Avoid "all of the above" and "none of the above". Avoid negative stems; if
  one is unavoidable, emphasise the negative word (لَيْسَ / **bukan**).
- Vary the position of the key across items. Do not let the longest option be the answer.
- Give no grammatical clues: if the stem needs a feminine noun, all options are feminine nouns.

Fill-in and rearrange items:
- Each blank must have exactly one answer that fits both grammatically and in
  context. If two answers fit, rewrite the item or list both in the key.
- For rearrange items, Arabic word order is flexible (VSO and SVO). List every
  grammatical order in the key, or choose items with only one natural order.

## Distractor design for Arabic

Make the distractors differ from the key in one feature that the lesson targets:

| Feature | Key | Distractors |
|---|---|---|
| gender | هٰذِهِ غُرْفَةٌ | هٰذَا غُرْفَةٌ / هٰذَا غُرَفٌ |
| plural pattern | بُيُوتٌ | بَيْتَاتٌ (overgeneralised sound plural) / أَبْيَاتٌ (real word, different meaning: verses) |
| i'rab | فِي الْبَيْتِ | فِي الْبَيْتُ / فِي الْبَيْتَ |
| verb person | هِيَ تَطْبُخُ | هِيَ يَطْبُخُ / هِيَ نَطْبُخُ |
| meaning field | dapur → مَطْبَخٌ | حَمَّامٌ / حَدِيقَةٌ (same theme) |

Wrong forms may appear only as distractors or in a clearly marked "find the
error" task. Never use them in model texts, examples or the vocabulary list,
because students may memorise them.

## Cognitive level and HOTS

- Low level (LOTS): C1 remember, C2 understand, C3 apply.
- HOTS: C4 analyse (compare, find implied meaning), C5 evaluate (judge,
  justify), C6 create (design, produce). HOTS items use a contextual stimulus,
  such as a short text, picture, floor plan or schedule, and ask learners to
  transfer what they learned to a new situation.
- Beginner lessons stay mostly at C1–C3 but add one light C4 or C6 task, for
  example "Lihat denah rumah ini. Tulis 3 kalimat tentang letak ruangan", or
  "Rancang kamar impianmu dan jelaskan kepada temanmu".
- Intermediate and advanced lessons include at least one HOTS task with a
  stimulus.

## Rubrics for speaking and writing

Default analytic rubric with 4 levels (4 = sangat baik, 3 = baik, 2 = cukup,
1 = perlu bimbingan). Adapt the descriptors to the task.

Speaking (كَلَامٌ)

| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Task completion (isi) | all required points, extra detail | all required points | some points missing | few points, off-task |
| Vocabulary | uses target words accurately and varies them | mostly accurate | limited, some wrong words | very limited |
| Accuracy (gender, i'rab, verb form) | almost no errors | minor errors, meaning clear | frequent errors, meaning sometimes unclear | errors block meaning |
| Pronunciation and fluency | clear sounds (ح/ه، ع/ء…), natural pace | mostly clear, short pauses | several sound errors, long pauses | hard to understand |

Writing (كِتَابَةٌ)

| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Task completion | all points, well organised | all points | some points missing | few points |
| Vocabulary | accurate and varied target words | mostly accurate | limited | very limited |
| Grammar and agreement | almost no errors | minor errors | frequent errors | errors block meaning |
| Spelling and harakat | correct letters, hamza, ة, harakat as required | few slips | many slips | hard to read |

Score = (total points ÷ maximum points) × 100. State the minimum mastery
threshold only if the teacher provides it. Do not invent one.

## Answer-key format

- Put it after all student material, under the heading مِفْتَاحُ الْأَجْوِبَةِ.
- Use the same exercise numbers and item numbers as the student section.
- Closed items: the answer only. Listening items: also the teacher's script.
- Open items (speaking and writing): a model answer, the accepted variants, and
  the rubric reference.
- Add a one-line explanation for items that test the grammar point, e.g.
  "هٰذِهِ karena حَدِيقَةٌ muannats (ة)".
